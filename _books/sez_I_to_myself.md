## Summary

*Sez I to Myself: The Collected Essays of Frank and Malachy McCourt* brings together a collection of essays and reflections by the Irish-American brothers Frank and Malachy McCourt.

The book explores a wide range of subjects, including family, identity, education, religion, immigration, writing, and everyday life. Through personal experiences, humor, and thoughtful observations, the brothers reflect on the people, places, and events that shaped their understanding of the world.

Rather than following a single narrative, the collection offers different perspectives on life through independent essays. Their storytelling combines humor, honesty, nostalgia, and social commentary, providing insights into both personal experiences and broader human concerns.
