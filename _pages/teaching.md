## Teaching Overview

My teaching experience spans undergraduate computer science and engineering courses, with an emphasis on hands-on laboratory instruction, programming, data structures, operating systems, databases, numerical methods, system analysis and design, computer vision, and digital logic.

I am currently a **Graduate Teaching Assistant at the University of Kansas**, supporting **EECS 140 — Introduction to Digital Logic Design**. Previously, I served as a **Lecturer at BRAC University**, where I taught **40 undergraduate sections across 6 academic semesters**.
